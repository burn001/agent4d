All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) (0.x until a stable 1.0).

## [Unreleased]

## [0.5.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- Long jobs started outside Claude Code (a measurement run launched with `Start-Process`, a detached batch script) can now be shown next to their project. Register one with `agent4d job add --log <file> [--pid N] [--pattern RE] [--end <marker file>] [--expect 2h]` (the PID is found from the log path when omitted), which writes one small descriptor per job to `<state folder>/jobs/`. The server tails the log and draws the job as a job node under the project's live main session, with `n/total` progress (any `12/40`-style counter by default, or your regex), rate and time left on the floor progress strip, or elapsed time against the expected duration when the job prints no progress. It ends when the marker file appears (done), the process is gone (done, or failed after a Traceback/error line or a missing promised marker), and the running server removes descriptors 12 hours after their job ended. Live only; `agent4d job list|find|rm|prune`.
- The auto camera now stays on a git action until it is done. When an agent starts a commit, push, pull, fetch, clone, merge, rebase, tag, PR create or PR merge, the camera frames that agent, its tool panel and label, and the remote-repository prop (the portal above the island in Flow, the satellite orbit in Planet, the dispatch pad in Voxel), and keeps it there until the call returns and the effect's afterglow has faded (3.6 s, shorter at fast replay speeds), then eases back. In Follow mode the tour pauses on that agent. The camera chip reads `git push` meanwhile. Manual control, an open details popup, Follow my prompts and the project or session filter still win; read-only calls and CI waits are not held, a hold ends after 20 s without a result, and actions that arrive while catching up, in a server batch, or at 8× replay and above are skipped. Flow, Planet and Voxel, 3D and 2D.
- Long-running commands carry a `meta.run` record for the viewer: while a foreground Bash/PowerShell call is open, the agent that made it gets `{kind:'fg', tool, since, lastOut, staleAfter, buffered}` (the oldest open call, cleared when it returns); background shells and detached processes get `{kind:'bg', since, lastOut, staleAfter, buffered}` plus `ended` and `rc` when they finish. `staleAfter` is the same adaptive quiet threshold as detached processes (at least 2 minutes) and `buffered` flags a command that has printed nothing yet and probably will not until the end, with the reason in `bufferWhy`: `python` (a Python run without `-u`/`PYTHONUNBUFFERED`), `pipe` (the last pipe stage holds everything until its input ends: `tail` without `-f`, `sort`, `wc`, `uniq -c`, PowerShell `Select-Object -Last`, `Sort-Object`, `Measure-Object`; `head` is not counted because it passes lines through at once) or `devnull` (output discarded or captured: `> /dev/null`, `Out-Null`, `X=$(…)`). Heredoc bodies, here-strings and quoted `|` are ignored. Updates that only move `lastOut` are sent at most every 5 seconds; replays rebuild the same record.
- Progress counters now also come from foreground shell output: `n/total`, `n%`, tqdm bars and `::agent4d` protocol lines printed by a long foreground command (live) become a Progress job under the agent that ran it, and the job ends when the call returns.
- Background shells that send their output to a log file (`> run.log 2>&1`, `| tee run.log`, `Tee-Object`, `Out-File`) now read that file as a second output stream of the same node (live only), so the panel, progress counters and quiet detection work even though the task output file stays empty.
- Long-running shells no longer look like they are resting. Nodes running a command show small tags `running 3:42 · quiet 2 min | stale 4 min · output buffered? · rc N` (elapsed switches to whole minutes after 10 minutes; the buffered tag's tooltip explains why: `python -u` or `PYTHONUNBUFFERED=1` for Python, "the last pipe (tail etc.) holds the output until it finishes", or "output is being discarded"), an open shell panel shows elapsed time in its header and a progress ring after 30 seconds, a background shell's output panel stays as a small one-line state with elapsed time instead of folding away, running shells keep their labels (the three longest first), they look busy instead of idle (stalled ones turn stall orange), the auto camera gives them a bonus that grows with elapsed time, and the inspector gets a running-shell section (elapsed, last output, buffering guess, stall against its threshold, rc). Flow, Planet and Voxel, 3D and 2D, live and in replays (replays use the replay clock).

### Changed
- Special-interaction effects in 3D Flow are now readable at the default camera distance. Lines are drawn as 2 px round-capped strokes (3 px for the most active, focused or camera-target agents) instead of WebGL's fixed 1 px lines; test scoreboards, loop progress bands, database cylinders and remote tunnels moved from the agent's height (where the tool panels covered them) to the floor around the agent; small stations of the most active agents grow until they are at least 48 px wide on screen (up to 3×); the GitHub portal is only drawn while a git action or a CI failure glow is running; and every effect stays at least 3 seconds after it ends (remote 1.4 → 3.0 s, database 2.4 → 3.5 s, loops 2.0 → 3.0 s).
- The same readability fixes now reach 3D Planet and the Voxel office. In Planet, test scoreboards, loop progress bands, database cylinders and remote tunnels sit on a deck below the planet, on the camera side and outside its orbits, instead of being scattered as 1–3 px dots over the planet body, its orbit rings and labels; they are drawn at 0.7× the Flow size (planets sit closer together) and the most active, focused or camera-target planets still grow them to at least 48 px (up to 3×). In the Voxel office, the facility effects of the most active, focused or hovered agents (parcels, cards, drone, antenna dots, quality-check lamps and bars) grow up to 2× when the office is small on screen while the furniture stays put, thin pieces stay at least 2 px wide (3 px for those agents), effects keep full strength for the first 60% of their afterglow (at least 3 seconds, as in Flow) instead of fading from the moment they end, and the dispatch pad's corner lights are only on during a git action or a CI failure glow.
- Plan boards are twice as large in Flow, Planet and Voxel, 3D and 2D: text, progress bar, item strip and padding all scale together. They keep their place beside the anchor node (growing right and up), still hide only when fully off screen, and label avoidance and the 3D cut-outs for nodes in front follow the new size. The close-up size cap doubles too.

- Long-running jobs in Planet are no longer the smallest thing on screen. Each job probe now carries the same status glyph as Flow (dots, play, hourglass, check, cross, exclamation) and grows with how long it has run (log scale, from 0.42 to the background-command size after two hours). A job flies a transfer track around its owner: the part already flown is a bright wake that thickens toward the probe, the rest is a faint dotted path ending at an arrival gate labelled with the time left and the ETA (`~13m · 16:32`), and the departure gate shows the start time. A job whose progress is only an estimate from `--expect` draws its wake dashed and labels it `est. 4:32 of 4:20` (or `over estimate N m`); a job with unknown ratio shows a comet tail and a lap marker (one lap per ten minutes); a waiting job shows twelve ticks filling toward its re-check time; a stalled wake cools from its tail; a finished track folds into a closed ring and fades in three seconds; a failed one breaks at the probe. Up to four unfinished jobs per owner get a track, the rest keep the old short trail. Jobs registered with `agent4d job add` are drawn as mining platforms (the detached-process rig) with a gauge above the derrick showing `n / N`, rate, elapsed time and ETA, or a dashed `96% (est.)` bar. The launcher ring now shows its recent cycles (green done, red failed, grey stopped) and the current cycle's progress instead of three carriers, and a re-armed Monitor shows one tick per re-arm (up to twelve). 2D Planet draws the same tracks, glyphs, platforms and rings; reduce motion stops the sparkles, dash flow and gate flash.

### Fixed
- Flow 3D floor stations, especially the test scoreboard, still covered node labels on the lowest tier: only the centre of each tile or line was checked, so tiles enlarged to the 48 px screen minimum overlapped label text while their centres sat just outside. Tiles are now checked by their on-screen radius and lines as whole segments, and the score badge and other station text fade to 20% where they cross a node label (2D Flow and Planet badges too).
- An external job's progress band covered that job's own label (Flow and Planet). Band cells and lines that fall inside a node label are now drawn at 12% so the label stays readable.
- Right after opening the page an external job had no progress band until its next progress event (up to a minute), because the snapshot carries no band. The band is now seeded from the job's own progress when the node arrives.
- A finished external job lost its label in close-up once it had ended more than ten minutes ago. Job nodes close to the camera (body at least 4 px on screen) are now label candidates whether or not they have finished.
- Gauges of neighbouring platforms could sit on top of a node label in Planet; labels now move out of the way of gauge panels.
- 3D special-interaction effects ignored the system "reduce motion" setting (test failure shake, pass sparkle and other motion kept running); they now follow it like 2D does.
- Planet never read the system "reduce motion" setting at all, so neither its special-interaction effects nor its own planet motion (spin, orbits, sparks) followed it; it now does, like Flow. The Voxel office's special-interaction effects follow it too: drone rotors, flowing cards and parcels, antenna dots, blinking lamps and test sweeps stop and show a still state.
- Background shells whose session ended first no longer stay running or stale forever. Claude Code writes the closing `[exited with code N]` line, so when the session that started a task ends before it (a headless `claude -p` run finishing its turn, a killed session, a reboot), no marker or notice ever arrives. The server now treats the session as ended when its transcript closes with a `cost-state` record (not the one written by `/clear`, whose process keeps running) or, with no such record, when the session's process record in `~/.claude/sessions` is gone or its pid is dead (only for the `cli` and `sdk-cli` entry points, after 10 seconds without the record and 30 seconds of a quiet transcript). Its open background shells then end as stopped, reason `session-end` ("Cut off when its session ended (exit code unknown)"), at the later of the session's last record and the last output; a marker or notice that still arrives afterwards wins. Live and replay use the same rule; a session whose process is alive is never cut, however quiet.
- `window.__ccviz` lost `director`, `onEvent`, `panel` and the sound handles: a comment added with the event queue swallowed the rest of that line (headless checks and the media script use them).
- Bursts of events no longer freeze the viewer. The server now queues each live connection: repeated `node-update`s for the same node within 100 ms are merged (last value wins, `meta` merged per key, other events keep their order), a slow connection or tunnel is written in one go after `drain` instead of piling up in the socket, and a connection that falls more than 5,000 events or 4 MB behind gets a fresh snapshot instead. The browser applies live events within a per-frame budget (about 6 ms); when more than 200 are waiting it applies the older ones without effects or sound and shows `Catching up n` on the connection chip until fewer than 50 remain, and a hidden tab catches up quietly in the background. Replays are unchanged.
- During a burst the server now tidies events before sending them, so the browser receives far fewer. When a connection gets more than 50 events per second, it switches to burst mode: events are held for 250 ms (growing to 1 s while the burst lasts), tidied by state, and sent as a single `batch` message. Updates to one node become one update, a node added and removed in the same window is dropped, short tool calls that finish in the window become one `tool-summary` line ("Tools · n quick calls") with the last call kept for the hologram, output keeps the latest tail plus a line count, text and thinking keep the latest (thinking sums its characters), and user messages, results, conflicts, cross-session messages and anything with interaction metadata are always kept in order. Events that stand for several originals carry `coalesced: n` and play no sound or packet. Burst mode ends below 20 events per second. A slow connection or tunnel collects into the same window until it drains, so it gets tidied more. The screen can lag up to about a second during a burst, but the final state is the same as applying every original event (property-tested), and reconnecting with `since` works as before. Replays and the server's event buffer keep the original events. In a test with 20,000 events, the browser received about 145 events instead of about 17,000, and the longest frame dropped from 171 ms to 57 ms in 3D and from 110 ms to 23 ms in 2D.
- Agents running a long foreground command no longer turn idle after one minute: a node with an open shell call stays active (up to the call's timeout plus a minute), live and in replays. Background shells no longer turn idle after a quiet minute either; they wait for the adaptive quiet threshold.
- Background shells whose completion notice never arrived (or was only written as a queued notice) no longer stay idle forever: the task output's closing `[exited with code N]` line ends them (0 done, 130/137/143 stopped, others failed, exit code kept), and queued notices count as notices without being shown twice.
- tqdm-style progress that rewrites one line with carriage returns now shows as one updating line in the panel and drives the progress counter, instead of every update being glued into one long line.
- A command that ran in the foreground and then moved to the background now starts at the time it was launched, not when it moved.
- The artifact layer showed far more objects in Planet than in Flow for projects with many agents: Planet's round island is larger, so its 500 targets stayed one-per-file while Flow folded them into folder groups. Both views now switch to folder groups once a project has more than 150 targets (`ART.LOD_COUNT`), whatever the island size.
- Plan boards for phase design docs now list every phase, not only the first. A run that looked at the doc in pieces (`sed -n 1,66p`, a partial Read, a `grep` of headings) only showed the phases inside that piece, and a `grep -n '단계 . 상태'` chained after other commands on another `.md` file (`git status && wc -l SUPERVISOR.md && grep …`) was dropped because its lines carry no file prefix. Partial views now merge into the known plan without removing phases they did not show, and unprefixed status lines go to the one design doc in the command.
- Phase design docs are recognised in more shapes: `## Phase N` / `Step` / `Stage` headings, numbers like `1a` or `10`, table rows (`| 단계 N | name | ✅ |`), and status lines without bold. A phase heading with no status line is listed as pending (and a later partial view without status keeps the known status). The `**단계 N 상태**: ✅/◑/⏸` form works as before.

## [0.4.1] - 2026-10-26

### Fixed
- Picking one project (top project picker or `agent4d ctl scope project:…`) no longer hides most of it. A launcher started with `nohup … &` has no parent, so it is drawn as its own root inside the island; the project fade only exempted session roots (`s:…`), so the launcher and everything under it (runs, their subagents and jobs) faded out with the other projects and only the main agent was left. ## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- Detached processes: when an agent starts a program or script in the background from a normal shell call (`nohup … &`, `( … ) > log 2>&1 &`, `setsid`, `disown`, PowerShell `Start-Process`/`Start-Job`, `start /b`, `python -m …`, a venv `python.exe`), it now gets its own node under that agent instead of the agent just looking idle. Its log file (`> f`, `>> f`, `&> f`, `| tee f`, `-RedirectStandardOutput`, relative to any earlier `cd`) feeds the usual output panel. It ends on `EXIT n`/`rc=n` lines, a Python traceback, a kill command that names it, or long silence, and shows as stalled when the log stops growing for longer than its usual line gap. Short `sleep 1 &` calls and GUI launches (`start chrome`, `code .`) are ignored.
- Progress lanes: common progress lines (`[label] 12,345 units · 5546s · max 4.32s`, `n/total`, `n%`, `label: n`) are recognised without any rules once they rise twice in a row, and become a "Progress" job with one counter per label, rate, ETA (when there is a total) and a 0–1 "heat" that rises as the rate drops from its start or the per-unit time grows. Protocol lines and `jobs.json` rules still win.
- Watching: when another shell call or Monitor reads or waits on that log (or its output folder), a new `watch` event links the reader to the process (peek or wait), and progress lines in what it saw count too. Replays place log lines at the times they were seen. `?mock=1` and `--demo` include a two-lane job that slows down, is checked every few seconds and finishes, plus one that fails fast.
- Planet (3D and 2D) draws detached processes as a mining rig on a small asteroid beside the agent that started it: the auger spins with the rate, slows and stutters as heat rises while its bore glows amber to vermilion, ore chunks ride one conveyor orbit per progress lane into a cargo ring around the agent (a big chunk per progress line), a hologram meter shows rolling counters, rate, longest unit time, heat and ETA (just the big number from afar), and watching shows as a waiting orbit with a probe or a scan beam, with the agent's line reading "Supervising · script · count".
- Flow (3D and 2D) draws detached processes as a standing machine beside the agent that started it: a rotor spins on top (slowly when the log stalls, with a blinking warning dot, and spins down when it ends with a green, red or grey wave), the front panel rolls one odometer counter per progress lane with a rate sparkline, heat bar and ETA (one big number from afar), and the body warms from teal through amber to vermilion as heat rises (heat haze in dark, deeper ink in light). Each new progress line pops a small tick out along the dashed spawn line, watching shows as a scan sweep from the watcher, a waiting ring around the machine and a check on the panel, the watcher's line reads "Supervising · script · count", hovering shows a small card, and the click card (every view) gets a progress-lane table plus the log path, watch count and end reason.
- Voxel office (3D and 2D) draws detached processes as a production line in the office of the agent that started it: a machine with a spinning flywheel, chimney steam and a beacon (amber when warm or stalled, red when hot or failed, green when done), one conveyor per progress lane running at the rate, a crate for each progress line that rides to a pallet stacked as a compressed tally (bundles and singles), and a forklift that carries the pallet away on a new pass or when the job ends. The progress job becomes a scoreboard above the machine with rolling lane counters, rate, longest unit time and ETA. When the agent checks the log, its character walks to the machine with a clipboard, reads the board and ticks it, or waits with arms folded (too-frequent peeks, fast replay or other rooms show a dotted look line from the desk instead).
- Remote control from the PC running the server: `agent4d ctl <command>` changes every open viewer (or one picked with `--client`) — theme, view, 2D/3D, project or session scope, what is shown, sound and volumes, music, language, camera, focus, live pause, replay, layers, weather, grid, noise, panel, timeline, popups, reload and on-screen messages — exactly as if picked in the menus (saved, or `--temp` for this visit only). `ctl clients` lists open viewers; each viewer answers with the result, and the CLI reports how many applied and why others did not (including "click the page once" for sound). Sending works only locally (loopback, no relay/tunnel/proxy headers, admin secret); remote viewers only receive. ⚙ gets "Screen name" and "Remote control: Accept/Ignore".

### Changed
- Flow 3D: the "me · user" console is now a solid terminal instead of a flat outline — a hexagonal base with a glowing ring, a neck, a screen panel tilted back with a bezel and a back cover, dark glass that lights up by state (no messages, main working, answered, asking me, interrupted) and a status light on the bezel, with the same floor shadow and dashed stem line as the hexagonal nodes. The text lines and the name tag stay as before; the gold beam and ribbon of a user message now leave from the screen. Hover, click card, light theme, reduced motion and far-away simplification keep working. 2D, Planet and Voxel are unchanged.
- Signal degradation starts later: film grain from 30% of the context window (was 20%), scanlines 50% (40), chromatic aberration, glitches and muffled music 70% (58), datamosh, hum and "Consider wrapping up" 90% (75). 3D shader, 2D overlay, text glitches and the hint text all read the same stage constants.
- Flow 3D: the launcher device (e.g. `relaunch.sh`) now stands upright instead of lying flat. Its front (recent run bands, progress bar and light) faces the camera and turns only when you rotate the view a lot; beams to its runs leave from its bottom edge.

## [0.3.7] - 2026-10-26

### Fixed
- `agent4d start` no longer keeps an old server running after the install was updated without `agent4d update` (for example with `git pull`). It compares the running server's version (`/healthz`) with the installed `package.json`; if they differ, it restarts the server, the relay and the watcher on the new code (the tunnel is reused). Before, the viewer kept showing the old version and server-side fixes did not take effect until a manual `stop`.

## [0.3.3] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- Live output of foreground shell commands. Claude Code writes the output of a running foreground Bash/PowerShell call to `tasks/<random id>.output` in the session temp folder and deletes it when the call ends, and that id never appears in the transcript, so long commands (for example a 10-minute test driver that prints a line per step) used to look frozen until they finished. The server now picks up unknown `.output` files in the session's task folders, attaches each one to the open (no result yet) Bash/PowerShell call of that session or its subagents whose start time is closest, and streams new lines to that call's tool panel as they arrive. The link ends when the result arrives, the file disappears or the turn ends. Live only; replay and archive are unchanged. Also works for sessions after `/clear`, whose temp folder carries a different session id. The first line may appear up to about 2 seconds late.

### Changed
- Work-target layer: temporary files no longer crowd out real work. Files under folders such as `tmp`, `temp`, `scratch`, `scratchpad`, `.cache` or `__pycache__` (and `*.tmp`) are marked temporary and are dropped first when a project reaches the 500-target limit. When four or more temporary files share a folder, they are folded into one group (count, five most recent files and the combined activity log in its card). Projects driven by autonomous loops that write many scratch files per round now show mostly their real code and documents. Same rule in Flow, Planet and Voxel Office.

## [0.3.2] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- `agent4d update`: `update --check [--json] [--force]` asks GitHub for the latest release (one unauthenticated request, cached for 24 hours in `update-check.json`; offline or API errors never fail). `update [--yes]` updates a git clone with `git pull --ff-only` after checking that it is clean, on `main` and can fast-forward, refreshes a copied skill in `~/.claude/skills/agent4d` (a linked skill is left alone; replaced files are kept as `*.bak`) and restarts a running server with the same options. Clones whose `origin` is not this repository are left untouched.
- The server checks for a new release at start and once a day; the ⚙ menu then shows a small dot and "vX.Y.Z available" next to the version, linking to the release page. Turn the check off with `"updateCheck": false` in `config.json` or `AGENT4D_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1` (no network request at all).
- The bundled skill checks for updates after finding the install, tells you about a new version and asks before updating (`AGENT4D_AUTO_UPDATE=1` updates without asking). New triggers: "update agent4d", "agent4d 업데이트".
- README: link the skill folder instead of copying it (`ln -s`, Windows `mklink /J`), new "Updating" section, and the update check is listed under security and privacy.

### Fixed
- Automatic camera in the overview: the "Overview" and project-island shots of "Framing: follow" (and clicking an island) no longer frame whole islands, their floor plates and the work-target layer. Like "Framing: all", they fit the active agents with their tool panels and labels, so agents appear much larger; the island box is used only when an island has no visible agents. "Framing: follow" also stops lingering on a project whose short turn has ended: idle agents score zero, and within about 3 seconds the camera moves to an island that is still working (for example a runner in another project). Same in 3D and 2D for Flow, Planet and Voxel Office.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- Job objects: work that a background command, monitor or loop runner *reports* in its output (a remote nightly run, a measurement chain, a detached wait) now gets its own small node under that stream's owner. Same job id = same node (a re-armed monitor keeps the node), states queued · running · waiting · done · failed, plus "stale" when the heartbeat stops for longer than the threshold (judged with the view clock, so replay matches live). Phase is shown as the subtitle, with progress n/total, re-check time and exit code as tags. Only the 3 most recent finished jobs per owner stay visible.
- Line protocol: any script can print `::agent4d {"id":"nightly","state":"running","phase":"tests","progress":"96/101"}` (a relay prefix before it is fine). Fields: id, title, state, phase, progress, host, heartbeat, until, rc, staleSec, stale, note.
- Fallback rules for output without the protocol: regular-expression rules in `<state folder>/jobs.json` or `<project>/.agent4d/jobs.json` (per project, per stream kind/label/command, or for an extra log file that is tailed read-only while live). Events start · phase · progress · wait · beat · stale · end · fail with named groups (time, phase, n, total, until, rc, …). Protocol lines win over rules on the same stream. Rules are re-read every 5 seconds.
- Drawn in every view: Flow 3D (small hex chip with progress arc, hourglass while waiting, broken pulse when stale), Planet 3D (tiny probe with a state ring and orbit trail), Voxel Office (kanban cards on a board next to the owner's machine), and the matching 2D views. The inspector shows state, phase, progress, host, last heartbeat, re-check time, exit code, source and the last line; an owner's card lists its jobs. The owner's hologram tail panel highlights the line that produced each job.

## [0.1.3] - 2026-10-25

### Fixed
- Flow 3D: the leftmost cell of a launcher's recent-run band flickered with noisy streaks. The shader read the per-instance band code and cell count through interpolated varyings and used them with exact comparisons, so tiny interpolation errors (a count of 4.9999, a code of 936.9998) changed the cell's colour pixel by pixel and frame by frame. Integer values are now rounded before decoding and the "no run in progress" check has a margin. The same rounding is applied to the voxel hologram panel line pattern.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-25

Initial public release, published as **Agent4D** — your coding agents in 3D space + time. The project was developed as cc-agent-viz; the command is now `agent4d`, and `ccviz` keeps working as an alias. The state folder stays `~/.cc-agent-viz/` (with the older `~/.cc-session-viz/` fallback). Licensed under the Apache License 2.0 (an earlier snapshot of this release was briefly published under MIT).

### Added
- Local, read-only live viewer for Claude Code sessions: main agent, subagents, workflow agents, nested `claude -p` runs, background shells and monitors as one live tree, streamed over Server-Sent Events.
- Three views (Flow, Planet, Voxel Office), each in 3D and a lighter 2D mode; all-projects overview with an automatic camera tour; replay at 0.5×–32× with a read-only archive; plan board; context and usage-limit gauges; synthesized background music and sound effects; English and Korean UI; optional remote access through a Cloudflare tunnel and Worker.
- Work-target layer (files, outputs, repositories, databases, remotes, services) under each project, with per-verb interaction scenes.
- Planet view: agents drawn as watch tourbillons. The main agent is a gyro tourbillon whose frame and gears read first: a polished steel cage with chamfered (anglage) bridges over a fixed gold fourth wheel with 60 teeth and five cut-out spokes; the steel escape pinion rolls on that wheel at the true gear ratio and turns the hooked 15-tooth escape wheel, which ticks once per beat with a small recoil; the pallet lever flips with ruby stones; a tilted inner cage turns faster the other way around a gold balance wheel with timing screws and a blue Breguet-overcoil hairspring. Metals use a small environment map with a soft rim light in the dark theme and an ink outline in the light theme; the planet body shrinks to a core so the movement leads. Subagents wear a simpler cage from the same family (one instanced mesh for the whole scene). The beat follows the agent state (fast while using tools, a slow tick when idle, stopped when done) and eases between states. Level of detail keeps the ring and teeth silhouette at a distance. 2D draws the same movement from above.
- Human prompts: the camera first moves to the main agent that receives the prompt (⚙ "Follow my prompts", on by default; skipped while you steer the camera, when paused, or at high replay speed), then energy gathers at a random point on the screen edge and flies in as one smooth comet-like beam that curves out and homes in, with a trail of constant on-screen width; it lands with a ripple. If the main agent is off screen, the beam heads for that edge. Drawn as a screen-space overlay shared by all views and 2D; reduced motion shows a short straight beam. Matching soft "sss → whoosh → tick" sound.
- Tool hologram panels are clickable: hover shows "tool · summary", click opens the node card at that tool call; far-away panels keep a small tool name tag.
- Version shown by `ccviz --version`, `ccviz status` and at the bottom of the ⚙ menu (read from `package.json`, exposed on `/healthz`).
- `docs/ADAPTERS.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md` and an adapter request template for community adapters for other harnesses.
- README loops for each view (`docs/media/*.gif`) next to the screenshots, and an English canonical README with a Korean translation.
